Process

From first conversation to launch.

Four phases keep every project clear, collaborative and on track.

01

Phase 01Discovery

Understanding the business, goals, audience, and project requirements.

  • Kick-off conversation about goals and audience
  • Review of content, references and constraints
  • Agreement on scope and next steps
02

Phase 02Design

Developing the visual direction, layout, typography, and user experience.

  • Structure and page layouts
  • Typography, colour and visual direction
  • Review and feedback on the design
03

Phase 03Development

Transforming the approved design into a responsive, functional website.

  • Clean, semantic HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Responsive behaviour on every screen size
  • Interactions and animation where they help
04

Phase 04Delivery

Testing, optimization, final revisions, and website handover.

  • Cross-browser and device testing
  • Performance and accessibility checks
  • Final revisions and handover
